# 0002 Progressive Disclosure & Statutory Fee Ledger

We decided to structure client onboarding as a 4-stage progressive disclosure engine that explicitly itemizes Government Statutory Fees separately from Klay Legal Advisory Retainers in real-time. In-progress states persist locally via `localStorage` and serializable URL hashes without requiring upfront account creation, allowing prospective founders to collaborate with board members and legal advisors prior to formal submission.
