# 0003 Programmatic Compliance Triage & Due Diligence Tiers

We decided to implement an automated compliance risk classification engine directly within the client onboarding process, categorizing each Intake Dossier into one of three Due Diligence Tiers (Tier 1: Standard, Tier 2: Regulated, Tier 3: Enhanced Due Diligence). High-risk corporate activities, complex foreign holding structures, and regulated financial domains are automatically tagged for Senior Counsel review prior to client consultation, ensuring regulatory compliance and safeguarding UAE licensing timelines.
