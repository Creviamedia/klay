## Platform

web

## Users

- **Primary:** International founders, high-net-worth investors (HNWIs), enterprise executives, and family offices establishing or relocating commercial operations and assets to Dubai and the wider UAE.
- **Situation:** Navigating complex cross-border taxation (UAE 9% Corporate Tax, ESR), multi-jurisdictional licensing options (Dubai Mainland DED vs. 40+ Free Zones vs. Offshore), Tier-1 corporate bank account compliance, and asset succession.
- **Job to be Done:** Establish a resilient, legally watertight corporate vehicle with guaranteed banking clearance and bulletproof estate/succession governance.

## Product Purpose

Klay Consultants provides premier corporate structuring, company incorporation, banking pre-clearance, and wealth governance in the UAE. Its purpose is to eliminate bureaucratic risk and compliance failure by replacing commoditized package brokering with institutional legal rigor. Success means a foreign enterprise or family office is operational in the UAE with an active commercial trade licence, an approved corporate bank account, and legally binding DIFC/ADGM wills in place.

## Positioning

**Backed by Legal Experts, Not General Sales Consultants.**

While standard business setup agencies in Dubai employ general sales agents or MBA consultants who sell commoditized off-the-shelf packages, Klay Consultants is directly backed by **Klay Legal** (a dedicated corporate law firm within the Klay Group of Companies) and **Klay HR**. 

The positioning contrast is unequivocal:
> *You can either take commercial setup advice from a general sales broker, or engineer your corporate foundation directly with a team of legal experts.*

This ensures every memorandum of association, shareholder agreement, and licensing decision is fortified against regulatory audits, bank rejection, and future shareholder disputes.

## Operating Context

- **Jurisdictions:** Dubai Mainland (Department of Economy and Tourism - DED), Free Zones (Meydan, IFZA, DMCC, JAFZA, DSOA), and Common Law Financial Centres (DIFC, ADGM, RAK ICC).
- **Banking Reality:** Navigating Central Bank of the UAE regulations, KYC/UBO disclosures, and strict anti-money laundering (AML) controls across Tier-1 institutions (Emirates NBD, Mashreq, First Abu Dhabi Bank, Wio).
- **Tax & Compliance Environment:** UAE Federal Decree-Law on Corporate Tax (9%), Economic Substance Regulations (ESR), Ultimate Beneficial Ownership (UBO), and VAT compliance.
- **Ecosystem:**
  - **Klay Consultants:** Corporate structuring, trade licensing, jurisdictional selection, and banking dossier prep.
  - **Klay Legal:** Shareholder agreements, regulatory compliance, DIFC/ADGM wills registration, estate succession, and corporate governance.
  - **Klay HR:** Golden Visas, investor and employment residency quotas, Emirates ID, and executive mobility.

## Product Principles

1. **Legal Expertise Over Sales Brokering:** Every structural recommendation is grounded in UAE federal decree-law and legal precedent, backed by Klay Legal.
2. **Structure Precedes Licensing:** Solve tax residency, operational scope, and banking compliance before filing a single government document.
3. **Radical Statutory Transparency:** Itemize government fees, municipal charges, and visa allocations line-by-line with zero hidden broker markups.
4. **Sovereign Asset Ring-Fencing:** Integrate common law estate planning (DIFC/ADGM wills) to safeguard foreign investor wealth from statutory forced heirship.
5. **High-Velocity Execution:** Streamline government approvals and biometric channels through dedicated Klay HR VIP conduits.
