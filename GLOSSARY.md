# Klay Consultants Domain Model

The sovereign corporate structuring, legal entity formation, and estate governance domain for UAE founders and international investors.

## Language

**Founding Jurisdiction**:
The specific regulatory territory within the UAE (Mainland DED, Free Zone, or Financial Centre such as DIFC/ADGM) governing an entity's commercial licence.
_Avoid_: Location, zone, area

**Statutory Fee Schedule**:
The transparent, non-markup government fees mandated by UAE regulatory authorities for licensing, establishment cards, and visas.
_Avoid_: Pricing, quote, cost sheet, package rate

**Substance Covenant**:
The regulatory requirements (physical lease, resident manager, operational activity) proving genuine commercial substance under UAE Economic Substance Regulations (ESR).
_Avoid_: Office requirement, physical presence

**Klay Legal Conduit**:
The direct procedural bridge connecting corporate formation clients to in-house legal counsel at Klay Legal for bylaws, shareholder covenants, and regulatory compliance.
_Avoid_: Lawyer referral, legal broker, partner attorney

**DIFC Will Registry**:
The common-law testamentary registration platform in the Dubai International Financial Centre that legally protects company shares, real estate, and digital assets.
_Avoid_: Dubai will, local inheritance plan

**Advisory Retainer**:
The professional fee charged by Klay Consultants and Klay Legal for bespoke structuring, regulatory drafting, and corporate governance.
_Avoid_: Service fee, agent markup, package cost, commission

**Visa Quota**:
The statutory allocation of UAE residency permits granted to an entity determined by its licensing authority and registered office premises.
_Avoid_: Visa limit, staff headcount, visa slots

**Ledger Summary**:
The verified, itemized breakdown generated at the conclusion of client intake contrasting statutory government dues against advisory retainers.
_Avoid_: Quote, cart, estimate, preliminary invoice

**Intake Dossier**:
The structured corporate profile and statutory qualification file compiled through the onboarding engine and transmitted for legal review.
_Avoid_: Lead record, submission, form entry, customer ticket

**Sovereign Reference**:
The unique institutional identifier (e.g. KLAY-2026-XXXX) assigned to an Intake Dossier upon verification and submission.
_Avoid_: Order number, ticket ID, lead ID, reference code

**Due Diligence Tier**:
The statutory risk classification (Tier 1: Standard, Tier 2: Regulated, Tier 3: Enhanced Due Diligence) governing counsel assignment and secondary approvals.
_Avoid_: Risk score, priority tag, lead score
